# Troubleshooting

**The Export button is greyed out.**
Make sure a video is loaded and an output folder is chosen. In Interval mode the interval must be more than zero; in Manual mode, capture at least one frame first.

**My video won't load.**
Stills From Video opens files that macOS itself can play. If the file doesn't play in QuickTime Player either, convert it to MP4 or MOV first.

**Some thumbnails are blank.**
Thumbnails are decoded in the background. On long videos or when zoomed in close, it can take a moment to fill them — pausing playback helps. Blank thumbnails never affect what gets exported.

**The keyboard shortcuts in the strip don't do anything.**
Those keys only work while the thumbnail strip has focus. Click the strip once, then try again.

**Q captured nothing.**
Captured frames go into the Manual list. Switch the mode to **Manual** to see and export them.

**Where did my files go?**
Into the folder shown under **Output folder**. Click **Show in Finder** after an export to open it.

**Something else is wrong.**
Choose **Help › Send Feedback…** to tell us about it.
