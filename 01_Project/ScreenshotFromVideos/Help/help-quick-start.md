# Quick Start

1. **Load a video.** Drag a video file onto the window, or click **Pick video…** (or choose **File › Open Video…**, Command-O).
2. **Choose a mode** under **Mode** in the right-hand panel:
   - **Interval** — a frame every so many seconds or frames.
   - **Manual** — only the frames you capture yourself.
3. **In Manual mode, capture frames.** Find a moment in the player or thumbnail strip and click **Capture this frame**, or press Q while the strip is focused.
4. **Pick an output folder** with **Change…** under **Output folder**. The app remembers it next time.
5. **Set options** if you like: burn in a timestamp, number the files, choose a format.
6. **Click Export** (Command-Return). Just above it, the panel tells you how many frames will be written.
7. When it's done, click **Show in Finder** to open the folder.

To start over with another video, click **Unload** under the player. This only removes the video from the app — the file itself is not touched.
