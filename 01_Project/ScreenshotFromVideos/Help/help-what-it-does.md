# What Stills From Video Does

Stills From Video turns a video into a folder of still images.

You can take a frame at a regular interval across the whole clip, or pick the exact moments you want by hand. Each still can carry a burned-in timestamp, and the files can be numbered so they sort in the right order in Finder.

## Why it exists

Many tools — including AI assistants like Claude — can read images but not video. When you want to show what happens in a screen recording, a handful of timestamped stills says it clearly. Stills From Video makes that a few clicks.

## What it works with

- **Input:** any movie file macOS can play, such as MP4 and MOV.
- **Output:** PNG, JPG, HEIC or WebP images, written at the video's full resolution.
- Your source video is never changed or moved.

Best results come from short clips of a few seconds to a few minutes. Longer videos work too, but the thumbnail strip takes longer to fill.
