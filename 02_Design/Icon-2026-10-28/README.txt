Review of v1 (historical candidate)
The orange landscape tile communicates a still image more clearly than the placeholder's
blank yellow card. Two broad frames make a more compact silhouette than the tall old strip.
The output follows the charcoal/orange family and has no text or extraneous symbols.
It is somewhat more dimensional and luminous than the intended flat-family target.
The tile rotates clockwise rather than the requested counterclockwise direction; this
does not change the metaphor. Treat it as a candidate, not an approved final icon.
PNG dimensions and presence of alpha were checked with sips. The 1024 master was
visually inspected. Dock-size optical acceptance and live app integration remain open.

Existing app assets, source, active plan, and prior ZIP were not changed.
No app build, launch, desktop control, or commit was performed.

v1/v2 and all prompts for provenance. Design acceptance does not close pending Dock-size
or live app-integration checks. Next session returns to performance acceptance first.
