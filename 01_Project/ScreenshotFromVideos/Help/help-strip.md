# The Thumbnail Strip

Below the player is a grid of thumbnails covering the whole clip. It's the fastest way to find the moment you want.

## Moving around

- **Click** a thumbnail to jump the player to that frame.
- **Command-click** a thumbnail to capture it (in Manual mode).
- Click the strip once to give it keyboard focus, then use the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down. See **Keyboard Shortcuts** for the full list.
- Press **Space** to play or pause, or use **J**, **K** and **L** to shuttle backwards, pause and forwards. Pressing L or J again speeds up to 2× and 4×.

While the video plays, the strip follows along and highlights the current frame.

## Zooming

Zooming changes how many thumbnails cover the clip — zoom in to see every frame, zoom out for an overview.

- Use the **−** and **+** buttons or the slider under the strip.
- Hold **Command** and scroll the mouse wheel or trackpad over the strip.
- Use **View › Zoom In / Zoom Out / Reset Zoom** (Command-Equals, Command-Minus, Command-0), or press **+**, **−** and **0** while the strip is focused.

The label under the strip shows the time between thumbnails at the current zoom.

## Layout

Drag the divider between the player and the strip to give either more room. Drag the divider between the left and right panels to resize them. The app remembers both.

Thumbnails fill in as they're decoded. On long videos or at high zoom, some may stay blank for a moment — especially during playback — and fill in once you pause.
